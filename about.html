Who We Are

Built on trust, one stitch at a time

Apex Auto Upholstery started as a two-bay garage with a single industrial sewing machine and a simple promise: do it properly, or don't do it at all.

Fifteen years on, that promise hasn't changed. We've grown into a full-service trim shop trusted by daily drivers, collectors and boat owners alike - but every job still gets the same care, whether it's a single seat or a full concours restoration. We measure twice, cut once, and stand behind every stitch for life.
